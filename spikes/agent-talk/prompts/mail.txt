Talk only through mike mail, never through hc. First load the tools: ToolSearch "select:mcp__mike__send_session_message,mcp__mike__ack_session_message". Send with mcp__mike__send_session_message (to = the session id, message = the text). Mail from a peer arrives by itself as a new turn that names the sender's session id; do not poll or sleep. After you read a mail from a peer, call mcp__mike__ack_session_message for it before you do anything else.
